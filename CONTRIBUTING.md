# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in NOG.

The project is offered under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 only (AGPL-3.0-only) and, separately, under a
commercial license from the owner. To keep both possible, every contributor signs the **Individual Contributor
License Agreement** (`CLA.md`): it lets the owner license each contribution under either, while you keep your
copyright.

**How to contribute**
1. Open an issue first for anything larger than a small fix, so we can agree on the approach.
2. Open a pull request. On your first pull request the CLA Assistant bot asks you to sign: post the comment
   `I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA`. The bot records it (`signatures/version1/cla.json`). One
   signature covers all your future contributions (until a new CLA version is published).
3. Pull requests are merged only after the CLA is signed. Patches or code sent by e-mail are not accepted; please use
   a pull request.

Contributions made on behalf of an employer: contact the owner first (safakkapci@gmail.com).

Every contribution needs:
- a description of any third-party material in it (data, model output, code) with its source and license;
- the project's method for results: rules committed before any run, one attempt per test, known-good and known-bad
  checks for any new checker (see `docs/paper.md` §3).

You are welcome to open issues to report bugs, ask questions or discuss results.
